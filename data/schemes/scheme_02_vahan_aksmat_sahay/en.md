# Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme

## Details
Launched in 2020, the Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme is implemented by the Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Gujarat. It aims to provide immediate medical assistance and financial support to road traffic accident victims so that timely treatment is provided and mortality is reduced. The scheme provides treatment packages from ₹1,000 to ₹45,000 for different accident-related injuries, including emergency procedures, surgery and hospitalization, at empanelled hospitals.

## Benefit
The scheme provides cashless medical treatment at empanelled hospitals. Treatment packages range from ₹1,000 to ₹45,000 and cover different types of injuries and emergency care.

**Mode of Disbursement:** Cashless treatment is provided at empanelled hospitals without upfront payment.

## Eligibility
- The applicant must be a victim of a road traffic accident.
- The applicant must require immediate medical treatment for injuries sustained in the accident.
- The applicant must receive treatment at a hospital empanelled under the Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme.
- A Medico Legal Case must be registered for the accident.
- Police intimation with acknowledgment must be available for the road traffic accident.

## Application Process
**Offline**

1. **Emergency Treatment at Empanelled Hospital:** In case of a road traffic accident, the victim should be immediately taken to the nearest empanelled hospital under the scheme for emergency treatment. The hospital provides immediate care according to the severity of injuries.
2. **Medico Legal Case Registration:** Hospital authorities ensure that a Medico Legal Case is registered with the local police station and coordinate with the police for accident and injury documentation.
3. **Treatment Package Assessment:** The treating doctor assesses the nature and severity of injuries and determines the appropriate package from the 27 available packages.
4. **Documentation Collection:** Hospital staff collect the required Medico Legal Case details, police intimation, before-and-after treatment photographs, medical records, investigation reports and hospital bills.
5. **Cashless Treatment Provision:** The empanelled hospital provides cashless treatment according to the approved package amount. No upfront payment is required for covered treatment.
6. **Claim Processing and Settlement:** The hospital submits the claim and required documents to the scheme authorities. Payment is settled directly between the government and the empanelled hospital.
7. **Discharge and Follow-up Care:** After treatment, the patient is discharged with a discharge summary and follow-up instructions. Patients should retain copies of medical and treatment records.

## Documents Required
### At Time of Treatment
- Scanned copy of the Medico Legal Case Register showing details of injury.
- Police intimation copy with police acknowledgment.
- Two photographs showing the wound before and after treatment, with the patient's face clearly visible along with the treating doctor, including a patient photograph for identity.
- Out-Patient Record/Case Sheet showing the treatment given, including Operation Theatre notes if applicable.
- Investigation report.
- Hospital bill.

### Package-Specific Additional Documents
- **Fracture cases:** X-rays to rule out fracture and X-rays showing the fracture before and after stabilization.
- **Chest injuries:** Chest X-ray before and after intercostal drainage.
- **Abdominal injuries:** Blood investigation, chest X-ray, X-ray of erect abdomen, ultrasound, Computed Tomography scan report if required, and Electrocardiogram with report if required.
- **Spine injuries:** X-ray spine anterior-posterior/lateral and Computed Tomography/Magnetic Resonance Imaging.
- **Head injuries:** Computed Tomography scan report and other relevant investigations.

## FAQ

**Q1: What is the highest amount of financial coverage available for the most severe road accident injuries under this medical assistance program?**  
A1: The maximum treatment package available is ₹45,000 for Pelvic Injury-External Fixation with Implant, which includes stabilization with external fixation and haemodynamic stabilization.

**Q2: Do I need to pay any upfront costs at the hospital when I receive emergency treatment for my road accident injuries?**  
A2: No. Empanelled hospitals provide completely cashless treatment, so neither you nor your family members need to make upfront payments for covered treatments.

**Q3: What specific legal documentation must be completed at the hospital before I can receive treatment benefits under this accident support scheme?**  
A3: A Medico Legal Case must be registered with the local police station, and a police intimation copy with police acknowledgment is required for the road traffic accident.

**Q4: How much financial assistance is available for treating simple wounds and basic injury care following a minor road traffic accident?**  
A4: Package 1 provides ₹1,000 for treating simple wounds, including tetanus vaccination, analgesics, antibiotics, general condition check, cleaning, suturing and dressing.

**Q5: What additional documentation do I need to provide if my road accident resulted in fractures requiring treatment under this scheme?**  
A5: For fracture cases, X-rays to rule out fracture and X-rays showing the fracture before and after stabilization are required, along with the standard documentation.

**Q6: Can I receive treatment at any hospital for my road accident injuries, or are there specific hospital requirements under this program?**  
A6: You must receive treatment at a hospital empanelled under the Vahan Aksmat Sahay Scheme to be eligible for benefits.

**Q7: What is the coverage amount available for head injury cases that require Intensive Care Unit stay with ventilator support?**  
A7: Package 21 provides ₹15,000 for head injury with Intensive Care Unit stay, including ventilator support.

**Q8: How many units of blood transfusion are covered under this scheme, and what is the financial assistance provided per unit?**  
A8: Blood transfusion is covered at ₹1,500 per unit, with a maximum admissible limit of 3 units under Package 23.

**Q9: What specific photographic evidence must be submitted as part of my treatment documentation for claiming benefits under this medical assistance program?**  
A9: Two photos showing the wound before and after treatment must be submitted, with the patient's face clearly visible along with the treating doctor. A patient photo is included for identity.

**Q10: What is the financial coverage available for treating complex chest injuries requiring intercostal drainage on both sides with critical care?**  
A10: Package 13 provides ₹14,000 for bilateral intercostal drainage with Intensive Care Unit and ventilator support.

**Q11: Which government department is responsible for implementing this road traffic accident medical assistance program, and when was it launched?**  
A11: The scheme is implemented by the Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Gujarat, and was launched in 2020.

**Q12: What medical investigations and reports are specifically required for abdominal injury cases to qualify for treatment benefits under this scheme?**  
A12: Required investigations include blood investigation, chest X-ray, X-ray of erect abdomen, ultrasound, Computed Tomography scan report if required, and Electrocardiogram with report if required.

**Q13: How much financial assistance is provided for treating open fractures of large bones that require surgical intervention and internal fixation?**  
A13: Package 9 provides ₹40,250 for Open Reduction Internal Fixation-Large Bone, including internal/external fixation of compound fractures, flap cover and inpatient treatment.

**Q14: What is the coverage amount available for spine injury cases that require advanced imaging studies like Computed Tomography or Magnetic Resonance Imaging?**  
A14: Package 18 provides ₹7,000 for spine injuries requiring Computed Tomography/Magnetic Resonance Imaging.
