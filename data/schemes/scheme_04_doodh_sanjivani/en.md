# Doodh Sanjivani Scheme

## Details

The "Doodh Sanjivani Scheme" launched by the Women and Child Development Department, Gujarat, is a welfare initiative designed to improve the nutritional status of children and women in tribal and underdeveloped communities. The scheme benefits children aged 6 months to 6 years and pregnant and lactating mothers registered at Anganwadi centers. It covers 138 tribal and developing communities in 20 districts and is implemented through Anganwadi centers.

## Benefit

- **Nutritional Support for Children:** 100 ml of fortified flavored milk is provided 5 days a week to children aged 6 months to 6 years.
- **Nutritional Support for Mothers:** 200 ml of fortified flavored milk is provided 2 days a week to pregnant and lactating mothers.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be a child aged 6 months to 6 years or a pregnant or lactating mother.
- The applicant must be registered at an Anganwadi center.

## Application Process

Offline

1. Visit the nearest Anganwadi center.
2. Register yourself or your child with the Anganwadi center.
3. Provide the required documents.
4. Avail of the benefits during scheduled distribution days.

## Documents Required

- Birth proof for child.
- Identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, ration card).
- Pregnancy proof for pregnant mothers (e.g., medical certificate).
- Any other document, if required.

## FAQ

**Q1: What is the main objective of the "Doodh Sanjivani Scheme"?**  
A1: The scheme aims to improve the nutritional status of children and mothers in tribal and underdeveloped communities.

**Q2: Who is eligible for the scheme?**  
A2: Children aged 6 months to 6 years and pregnant or lactating mothers registered at Anganwadi centers are eligible for the scheme.

**Q3: What type of milk is provided under the scheme?**  
A3: Fortified flavored milk is provided under the scheme to enhance nutritional intake.

**Q4: How much milk do children receive under the scheme?**  
A4: Children aged 6 months to 6 years receive 100 ml of fortified flavored milk 5 days a week.

**Q5: How much milk do pregnant and lactating mothers receive?**  
A5: Pregnant and lactating mothers receive 200 ml of fortified flavored milk 2 days a week.

**Q6: Is there any cost to avail of the benefits of the scheme?**  
A6: No, the milk is provided free of cost to all eligible beneficiaries.

**Q7: Where can beneficiaries access the benefits of the scheme?**  
A7: Benefits can be accessed at Anganwadi centers in the participating districts.

**Q8: Does the scheme have an income limit for eligibility?**  
A8: No, there is no income limit for eligibility under the scheme.

**Q9: How can beneficiaries register for the scheme?**  
A9: Beneficiaries must register themselves or their children at their nearest Anganwadi center.

**Q10: Can children who are not registered at an Anganwadi center benefit from the scheme?**  
A10: No, only children registered at Anganwadi centers are eligible for the scheme.

**Q11: In how many districts is the Doodh Sanjivani Scheme implemented?**  
A11: The scheme is implemented in 20 districts of the state, covering 138 tribal and developing communities.

**Q12: What should beneficiaries do if they need more information about the scheme?**  
A12: Beneficiaries can contact their nearest Anganwadi center or the Child Development Scheme Officer for further information.
