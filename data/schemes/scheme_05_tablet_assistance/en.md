# Tablet Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students of 12th Standard

## Details

The “Tablet Assistance to Scheduled Caste Students of 12th Standard” scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat. Under the scheme, tablets are provided to students studying in the 12th standard who belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) category.

## Benefit

- Eligible 12th-standard students belonging to the Scheduled Caste category receive a tablet by paying a token fee of ₹1,000.

## Eligibility

- The student should belong to the Scheduled Caste category of Gujarat.
- The student should be studying in 12th Standard.
- There is no income limit.

**Note:** The student should have received a tablet from the Education Department by paying a token fee of ₹1,000.

## Application Process

Online

1. Visit the Digital Gujarat Portal: https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/
2. On the home page, click **Register** and complete the registration form.
3. Enter your mobile number, email address and password, then click **Save**.
4. After successful registration, log in using your mobile number/email ID and password.
5. Open **My Profile**, update your profile and fill in all mandatory information.
6. Click **Update Profile** and complete OTP verification.
7. Under Services, select **Scholarship Services**, then select **SC Scheme**.
8. Select the financial year in which you want to apply and click **Change Financial Year**.
9. Select the scheme, read the instructions carefully and click **Continue to Service**.
10. Complete the application form and upload all relevant documents.
11. Click **Save as Draft**, agree to the terms and conditions, and submit the application using **Confirm & Final Submit**.

**Note:** Students may also contact the principal of their school to avail the scheme benefits.

**Instructions**

- Photo uploading is mandatory for all applicants.
- The application is considered complete only after all sections are completed.
- Only one application per applicant is accepted. Multiple applications may be rejected.
- Incorrect or misleading information may lead to rejection.
- All required documents should be scanned clearly according to the instructions.
- Fill the application carefully because there is no provision to change the institution after successful submission.
- Unreadable uploaded documents may result in rejection.
- For students studying inside Gujarat, direct individual submission is not accepted. Students studying outside the state must apply online and submit the application to their native district officer.

## Documents Required

- Passport-size photograph
- Identity proof, such as Aadhaar Card
- Caste Certificate issued by the competent authority
- First page of bank passbook or cancelled cheque
- Break affidavit, if the break gap is more than one year
- 12th Standard marksheet, if available
- College/School Identity Card
- Any other documents required by the department

## FAQ

**Q1: Who administers the scheme?**

A1: The scheme is administered by the Director, Scheduled Caste Welfare, Government of Gujarat.

**Q2: What benefit is provided under the scheme?**

A2: Eligible students studying in 12th Standard and belonging to the Scheduled Caste category receive a tablet by paying a token fee of ₹1,000.

**Q3: What are the eligibility criteria?**

A3: The student must belong to the Scheduled Caste category of Gujarat and must be studying in 12th Standard. There is no income limit.

**Q4: Is there any income limit for eligibility?**

A4: No. There is no income limit specified for eligibility under this scheme.

**Q5: How can eligible students apply?**

A5: Eligible students can apply online through the Digital Gujarat Portal: https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/. Students may also contact their school principal for assistance.
