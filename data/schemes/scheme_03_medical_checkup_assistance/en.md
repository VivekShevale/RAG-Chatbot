# Full Medical Checkup Assistance Scheme- Gujarat Labour Welfare Board

## Details
The “Full Medical Checkup Assistance Scheme” is implemented by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board, Labour, Skill Development & Employment Department, Government of Gujarat. The scheme provides health check-ups for male Shramyogis above 45 years of age and female Shramyogis above 35 years of age working in the organized sector in Gujarat. Workers are eligible only when their Labour Welfare Fund has been paid regularly by their organization/unit for the last one year.

## Benefit
The scheme provides a complete medical check-up once a year for eligible Shramyogis. The fixed amount for a complete medical check-up is ₹1,950 per worker, and 25% of this amount, ₹487.50, is to be paid by the organization/company.

## Eligibility
- The worker (Shramyogi) must have Labour Welfare Fund contributions regularly paid by the organization/unit for the last one year.
- Male Shramyogis above 45 years of age are eligible.
- Female Shramyogis above 35 years of age are eligible.
- The worker must be working in the organized sector in Gujarat.
- The benefit is available once a year.
- After in-principle approval, the medical examination must be completed within 30 days with mutual agreement between the company and hospital.
- The Gujarat Labour Welfare Board is not responsible for side effects during or after the physical examination.
- The company seeking the benefit must upload the worker list in Excel format on the portal.
- Fees paid under the scheme are not refundable.
- The final decision regarding the scheme rests with the Welfare Commissioner, with jurisdiction at Ahmedabad.

## Application Process
Online

1. Visit the Sanman Portal: https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/
2. On the home page, under “Citizen Login”, click “Please Register Here”: https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/Home/Registration
3. Enter the Aadhaar Card Number, select the user type, and enter the Labour Welfare Fund Account Number.
4. Click “Fetch” and verify the displayed details.
5. Enter the required user details and password.
6. After successful registration, log in using the User ID and Password.
7. Select the scheme and carefully read its instructions.
8. Fill out the application form and upload all relevant documents.
9. Agree to the Rules & Regulations and submit the application. A confirmation email containing the application number will be sent to the registered email ID.

## Documents Required
- Passport-size photograph
- Copy of the identity card issued by the contractor to the worker/labourer
- Aadhaar Card of the labourer
- Labour Welfare Fund Account Number
- Bonafide Certificate: https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/ApplicationDocuments/GLWB/Bonafide.pdf
- Any other documents as required

## FAQ

**Q1: Who administers the Full Medical Checkup Assistance Scheme?**
A1: The scheme is administered by the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board under the Labour, Skill Development & Employment Department, Government of Gujarat.

**Q2: What is the objective of the scheme?**
A2: The objective is to provide comprehensive health check-ups for eligible male and female Shramyogis above the specified age thresholds.

**Q3: How much is the fixed amount for the medical check-up?**
A3: The fixed amount is ₹1,950 per worker for a complete medical check-up, with 25% of this amount, ₹487.50, to be paid by the organization/company.

**Q4: Who is eligible to avail of the benefits?**
A4: Workers whose Labour Welfare Fund has been regularly paid by their organization/unit for the last one year are eligible, provided male workers are above 45 years and female workers are above 35 years and they work in the organized sector in Gujarat.

**Q5: How often can a worker avail of the benefit?**
A5: The benefit is provided once a year to eligible workers.

**Q6: Is the Gujarat Labour Welfare Board responsible for side effects during or after the physical examination?**
A6: No. The Gujarat Labour Welfare Board shall not be responsible for any side effects during or after the physical examination.

**Q7: What is the timeline for completing the medical examination after in-principle approval?**
A7: The medical examination must be completed within 30 days after receiving in-principle approval, with mutual agreement between the company and the hospital.

**Q8: Are fees paid under this scheme refundable?**
A8: No. Fees paid under this scheme will not be refunded.

**Q9: How can one apply for the scheme benefit?**
A9: Applicants can apply online through the Sanman Portal: https://sanman.gujarat.gov.in/
